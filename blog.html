DAILY BLOG

Thinking out loud.
One insight at a time.

Some people journal. I write about what I learned today. It forces clarity — if I can't explain it simply, I didn't really learn it. These are the insights that stuck.

July 14, 2026

It's No Longer About Doing the Job

Here's a shift I've been thinking about: the value of a finance professional is no longer just doing the work well. It's knowing how to orchestrate AI to do 5 jobs at once — and knowing which parts still require human judgment. The orchestrator wins. The pure executor is in trouble.

July 13, 2026

Context Placement Matters More Than Context Volume

Counterintuitive thing I learned this week: giving AI more context doesn't always produce better output. It's like human cognition — dump too much, and the system focuses less on what's in the middle. Strategic placement of information beats information dumping every time. I've been restructuring all my prompts around this.

July 12, 2026

The Compound Effect of Custom Commands

Each reusable workflow-specific command I build saves time on the next engagement. And the next one. And the next one. After a month, I have a personal toolkit that makes me 3x faster than starting from scratch each time. The initial investment is 30 minutes. The payoff is permanent.

July 11, 2026

What Consulting Teaches You About AI Prompting

9 years of client work taught me one thing that transfers perfectly to AI: define the output before you start the work. Clients never want "analysis." They want a one-page answer to a specific question. Same with AI — the clearer your expected output, the better the result. Scope the deliverable first.

July 10, 2026

Why Finance Professionals Should Learn to Build, Not Just Use

There's a difference between using ChatGPT to summarize a document and building a pipeline where documents get parsed, data extracted, models populated, and reports formatted — automatically. The first saves you 10 minutes. The second saves your team 75+ hours a year. Both use AI. Only one changes the practice.

July 9, 2026

Plan Mode Changed How I Work

Biggest lesson from my Anthropic certifications: letting the AI think through its approach before executing produces dramatically better work. It's like the difference between a junior analyst who dives straight into Excel vs. one who sketches the model structure on a whiteboard first. The thinking time isn't wasted — it's where the quality lives.

July 8, 2026

The 14-Hour Problem

Something that used to take 14 hours — 10 to build the cash flow model, 4 for the valuation — now takes 45 minutes with the right agent architecture. I didn't get faster at Excel. I redesigned what "doing the work" means. The model still gets built. The valuation still gets run. But the human's job shifted from executor to reviewer.

July 7, 2026

Structured Finance Is Actually Perfect for AI

People think AI is for tech companies. But structured finance — waterfalls, tranches, collateral analysis, covenant testing — is all rules-based logic with massive document sets. It's almost embarrassingly well-suited for automation. The data is structured. The decisions are repeatable. The volume is high. This is where AI earns its keep.

July 6, 2026

The Best AI Interface Is the One That Disappears

Nobody on my team cares about the AI architecture behind a tool. They care that the report is done, accurate, and on time. The more invisible the technology, the more valuable it becomes. I'm learning to build tools that feel like magic to the user and boring infrastructure to the builder. That's the goal.

July 5, 2026

What 75+ Clients Taught Me About Pattern Recognition

After 75+ clients across banking, insurance, and asset management, you start seeing the same problems wearing different costumes. The specific numbers change. The regulatory framework shifts. But the underlying pattern — process inefficiency, information asymmetry, manual bottlenecks — is always the same. AI doesn't replace the pattern recognition. It accelerates what you do once you've spotted the pattern.

July 4, 2026

Happy 4th. Reflecting on the Journey.

Moved to the US at 17. Parents used most of their life savings to give me and my sister a shot at something bigger. 9 years later, I'm building AI systems at a Big 4 firm and thinking about what comes next. The urgency hasn't faded. If anything, watching how fast AI is moving makes me more convinced that standing still is the riskiest option.

July 3, 2026

Certifications vs. Building: Both Matter

Completed 8 Anthropic Academy certifications this week during firm shutdown. Here's the thing — the certifications alone don't make you an AI expert. But they fill knowledge gaps you didn't know you had. I've been building with AI for over a year, and the courses still taught me 3 critical things I was doing wrong. Experienced practitioners benefit most from beginner-level courses.

July 2, 2026

The Orchestrator Mindset

In consulting, the most valuable people aren't the ones who do everything themselves. They're the ones who know who to pull in, when, and how to make all the pieces fit. AI works the same way. The best multi-agent systems aren't built by the best coders — they're built by people who understand the workflow deeply enough to decompose it into specialized roles.
